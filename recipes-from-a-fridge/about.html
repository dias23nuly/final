Make more with what you have.

Our idea

Less waste.
More creativity.

Recipes from a Fridge is a student web project built around one question: what can I cook with the food already at home?

Select your ingredients, discover matching meals, explore instructions, and keep your favorites for later.

  • ✓ Ingredient-based matching
  • ✓ Simple recipes and clear instructions
  • ✓ Search, filters and favorites
  • ✓ Accessible light and dark themes

Built with HTML, CSS, JavaScript, jQuery and JSON. No account needed.
